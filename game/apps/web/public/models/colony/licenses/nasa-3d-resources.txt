Changes made in this project (2026-10-16):
- Draco mesh compression removed; geometry simplified to 35% of the original vertex count; textures re-encoded as WebP at 1024 px (habitat) and 512 px (rover).
- NASA insignia (meatball), NASA worm logotype, JPL logos and captions, US flags, the HDU programme mark and the mission name text were painted out of the textures. Nothing else in the textures was intentionally altered.
